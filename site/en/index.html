Key assessment

This issue clearly leans toward three parallel tracks: "enterprise deployment + model governance + embodied/agent toolchains." On one hand, enterprise-side narratives such as Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore, EKS, and SageMaker appear frequently, showing that generative AI is moving from pilots to process automation and platformization. On the other hand, OpenAI's Model Spec and misalignment monitoring emphasize that governance, alignment, and safety boundaries are becoming part of frontier model competition. At the same time, many research papers focus on agentic AI, verification, retrieval, scheduling, training unification, and interpretability, showing that the technical center of gravity is shifting from isolated capability gains to deployable, verifiable, and controllable system capabilities.
