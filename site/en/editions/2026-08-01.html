Key assessment

This issue shows AI entering a stage of “layered engineering competition”: model capabilities are still advancing, but the key factors determining deployment speed are increasingly catalogs, monitoring, policies, caching, evaluation, rewards, permissions, and cost structure.

Published08:12

Today's context

The dominant theme is “agent deployment and governance advancing together”: on one hand, enterprise products continue to ship capabilities such as agents, catalogs, observability, monitoring, caching, and permission control; on the other hand, the research community is systematically breaking down agent failure modes, evaluation reliability, inference cost, and security boundaries, showing that both the market and research are converging on making AI truly usable and controllable.
