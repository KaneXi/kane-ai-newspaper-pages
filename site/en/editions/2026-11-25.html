CoT-Proxy: Cross-Format Transfer for Distilling Long-Horizon Policies

The CoT-Proxy study on long-horizon policy distillation finds that effective long-horizon policies can be distilled from a strong teacher’s action trajectories, even without exposing chain-of-thought; combined with CoT proxies, the visible outputs of closed models can be turned into transferable training signals.

Delegent: A Multi-Agent Harness That Reduces Cost without Sacrificing Performance

Delegent proposes a multi-agent harness: a frontier model handles the main task, while easily decomposed subtasks such as search, testing, and code review are delegated to locally runnable SLM subagents, significantly lowering the frontier cost of long-horizon coding and agentic search.
